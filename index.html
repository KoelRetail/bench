    Before you start patching

    01

    Label them first

    From the outside the two ION 1200-S units are indistinguishable, and there are three identical Cisco switches side by side. The model assumes top = D01 and X01-1. Verify that once on the controller and on the switch, then put label tape on them.

    02

    The two bypass cables cross over

    Not 1 to 1 and 4 to 4, but D01 port 1 → D02 port 4 en D01 port 4 → D02 port 1. Turn the model side on and you can see the crossover. This is where it goes wrong in practice.

    03

    Which port number is the bypass pair?

    The drawing puts the dotted line around ports 1 and 4, but the note beside it says Port 3 & 4 are Bypass pair. Those contradict each other. Port 3 is the INET port on the same drawing, so check this before you patch — 1 and 4 were used here because that is where the lines on the drawing go.

    04

    POLR lands on port 4, but not everywhere

    The drawing says so itself: POLR Termination port 4 is new standard and it will be 9 in most of the existing sites. So check whether your site is already on the new standard before you patch the 5G into port 4.

    05

    The HA VLAN is in the same trunk list

    VLAN 20 is first in the trunk allowed list and is also labelled separately as HA Vlan. The drawing does not make clear whether that is one trunk cable or a second separate cable to the same ION. It is modelled here as one trunk — if it is two, say so and it can be changed.

    06

    The POLR only lands on one switch

    The 5G only goes to X01-1 port 4 and reaches X01-2 over the stack. If the stack drops, D02 loses its POLR path while D01 still has it. Keep that in mind when maintaining the stack.

    07

    Fill the AP ports in a fixed order

    The thirty Cisco 9120s lie stacked in pairs on the middle shelf of Bench 4 and go on X01-1 ports 11 to 20, X01-2 ports 11 to 20, and only if that is not enough on the third Cisco, ports 5 to 20. Keep that order and patch low to high, so later the port number tells you which AP belongs where.

    08

    The WTI and the third Cisco are not on the new drawing

    They are on the bench though, and the console server is wired to all three switches. Which WTI port goes to which switch is written down nowhere — put that in the WTI config as the port name, because when a switch is unreachable is exactly when you do not want to guess.
